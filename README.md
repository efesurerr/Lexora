LEXORA is a word contest where knowing the word matters, and so does how long it is. It runs on your phone with no install.

Example: The category is "rideable animal". Player A writes "horse", player B writes "donkey". "Donkey" is longer, so B wins the round.

How to play
Enter the player names, then choose the number of rounds (5, 8 or 12) and the answer time (no limit, 15 s, 30 s).
Each round shows a category. Players take turns with the phone and secretly type one word.
All answers are revealed. The player with the longest valid word wins the round.
Everyone tied on the longest valid length gets 1 point.
After the last round, the player with the most points wins.

If an answer is not in the built-in list, the players decide together: Accept or Reject.

Features
5 languages: Türkçe, English, Deutsch, Español, Français
Categories: 19 in Turkish, 14 in the other languages (animals, fruit, countries, jobs, sports, instruments, vehicles and more)
2-6 players, passing one phone around
Timed or untimed rounds
Music and sound effects switch on and off separately
Letter wallpaper, dark theme, phone-friendly layout
Single file (index.html), no install and no connection needed (a connection only loads the nicer fonts)
